## The front page

A newspaper front page, **THE WORLD**, drawn for 22 November 2026:

- **Lead — COP31 collapses in Antalya:** the UN climate summit ends with no
  agreement after the EU rejects a deal with no fossil fuels in it.
- **The Gulf — Iran strikes US bases and tankers** near the Strait of Hormuz
  and an industrial site in Bahrain; two injured, Tehran claims Patriot
  radars destroyed.
- **The Hague — Myanmar guilty of genocide:** the World Court rules Myanmar
  committed genocide against the Rohingya in 2017, the first state ever
  found responsible.
- **Ukraine — drone hits a Kyiv flat:** a Russian drone hits a block in
  Dymer (14 missing, 3 killed); a Kryvyi Rih missile kills 2 and wounds 60.
- **Markets — AI sell-off spreads:** the Nasdaq falls 5.3% and US stocks
  slide into correction.
- **West Bank — settlers torch cars** in four villages; Israel opens an
  inquiry.
- **Space — BepiColombo reaches Mercury** after an eight-year journey.
- **Indonesia — candy factory fire:** three dead, 36 missing.
- **Trade — EU–Indonesia deal** scraps tariffs on up to 98.5% of goods.
- **Also today —** Russian cargo ship Sparta II burns in the Gulf of Finland
  after a drone strike.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`), after checking the PNG
magic bytes and the 1 MiB size limit. The image is 1024×1024 and the program
writes identical bytes on every run.
